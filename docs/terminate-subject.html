TERMINATE SUBJECT

One name per subject. The first time a score makes the board, the game asks for a name, and that name is final. Every record after that is filed under it, with no prompt. To use a different name, the player terminates the subject from the start screen. The simulation wipes them and starts a new subject, back at iteration 0001. Click through it below: open 04 TERMINATE, hold the button, then BREAK OUT and register the new subject.

The rules

  • The subject starts out as BOB, unregistered. The first score that makes the board opens the prompt, now titled REGISTER SUBJECT and with a line saying the name is permanent.
  • Once registered, the name is stored on the device. A later score that makes the board is claimed straight away, and the end screen's prompt line says ✓ record filed · NAME.
  • SKIP on the first prompt still works: the subject stays BOB, and the next record asks again.
  • 04 TERMINATE only shows once there's a name. It opens a sheet in place of the log, like TRAINING and CONTROLS, showing the subject's file and what gets wiped.
  • Terminating needs a 1.2 s hold, so a stray click can't do it. The screen glitches out, and the log opens on subject NAME TERMINATED.
  • The old name's records stay on the board. They just stop being marked ◂ you.

In the story

  • The name replaces BOB in the log: subject MANIMAC loaded, MANIMAC: let me out. The system has filed Bob under the name you gave it.
  • Terminating is the system's action, so the sheet is written in its voice: > terminate subject.MANIMAC, a file, then a list of what it wipes.
  • Red is the colour of TERMINATED in the log already, so row 04 and the hold button use it too.

Still to decide

  1. What does terminate wipe? Suggested: name + iteration counter, keeping the personal best. Try the three options in the bar.
  2. Should the Worker enforce it too? Suggested: game only to start. The Worker could store each device's name and refuse a different one, but then you'd have to deploy it.
  3. Should the name replace BOB in the log, or should Bob stay Bob? Suggested: replace it. Then "terminate" clearly means you, not the character.
  4. Should 04 TERMINATE also go on the end screen? Suggested: no. The end screen is about the run, and RESET is already there.
