Where should the shell go?

The same shell (Shell v3) in four places, at the game's real start-screen proportions: 16:9, Bob zoomed in 2.8×. The claw intro plays with the game's timings, so how much arm you see is how much the game would show.

Why the arm is hard to see now

The arm comes in from far off to the left, and only its last stretch (the forearm and the claw) is ever on screen. With the panel on the left, that stretch is behind the panel, so you mostly see the jaws.

Anything that leaves the screen's left edge open between the edge and Bob shows the arm: a panel on the right, at the top, or at the bottom.

A

Right

The column from v3, mirrored. Bob charges at the shell; on BREAK OUT it slides off to the right, out of his way.

Good

  • The whole forearm shows, from the screen's left edge to Bob, as it did before the shell.
  • Bob runs at the system, and it gets out of his way: a strong break-out.
  • Same pieces as the top bar, stacked: log, menu, your best, podium, then this week as one list of ten.

Watch out

  • Tightest fit: the firewall has to end before the panel, so Bob stands a little left of his running spot (about 155 instead of 177 at 16:9).
  • The firewall almost touches the panel, so the data link comes over its top instead of into its side.

Effort

  • Low. Mirror the layout, aim Bob's start spot and the link the other way, slide out to the right.
B

Top

The shell as a bar across the top, like a console that has dropped down. The world below is untouched: Bob, the arm and the firewall sit where they do in the game. Three zones: logo and log, the menu with your best, and the leaderboard.

Good

  • The most arm of any layout, and Bob stays on his usual spot (no camera shift).
  • The data link drops straight down from BREAK OUT onto the firewall.
  • On BREAK OUT the bar retracts upward.

Watch out

  • The bar takes the top 38% (170 of 450 units at 16:9). It only covers sky: the firewall starts below it, with room for the data link.
  • The leaderboard is laid out wide: the all-time top 3 on one line, this week's top 10 in two columns of 5.

Effort

  • Medium. A new horizontal layout for the panel; the camera and arm stay as they were.
C

Bottom

The same bar as the top one, along the bottom like a dock. The camera lifts the world so the roof stays above it.

Good

  • Full arm, and the bar sits where a game's menu bar usually does.
  • On BREAK OUT it drops away and the camera settles back down.

Watch out

  • The roof would sit on the bar, so the camera has to lift the world a long way (about 100 units at 16:9): mostly sky above Bob, little building, and a big vertical camera move during the zoom-out.
  • The data link has to cross the roof to reach the firewall.

Effort

  • Medium. The horizontal layout plus a vertical camera offset on the start screen.
