Shell v3

Shell v2 (iteration A in start-screen-shell.html) with the improvements we listed, and the records changes from the console page.

What changed from v2

  1. Last run report is part of the log: the first entry is how iteration 0041 ended, its distance, and its score against your best.
  2. Real buttons: BREAK OUT is a big filled key with SPACE on it; TRAINING and CONTROLS are raised buttons side by side.
  3. No 01 / 02 / 03: the numbers suggested keys that don't exist.
  4. The whole leaderboard, no tabs: the all-time top 3, then this week's top 10 with the purge timer (hhh:mm:ss). At the bottom: YOUR BEST with your all-time rank, and your rank this week with the points to beat #1. Your row is tagged YOU.
  5. Data link: a dashed line runs from BREAK OUT to the firewall, ending at the TARGET tag. It lights up when you hover BREAK OUT.
  6. Smaller logo, so BREAK OUT is the boldest thing in the panel.
  7. Intro: the claw comes out of the panel's edge carrying Bob, sets him down and pulls back in. The log narrates it, and BREAK OUT and the data link wake up when it lets go.

Intro (↻ to replay)

  • Same timings as the game's START_PUSH: reach 0.12s, push 0.8s, retract 0.8s.
  • The panel is drawn on top of the world, so the arm is hidden while it's "inside" the shell. It needs to be opaque (it was 95%).
  • Log lines appear at the real times: [00.12] the claw has Bob, [00.92] he's on the roof, [01.72] it lets go and Bob speaks.
  • Until then BREAK OUT is dimmed, Bob's eye is still, and there's no data link. Clicking BREAK OUT early skips the intro, like pressing Space mid-push does now.
  • In the game, Bob's resting spot moves right (about 44% across) so he lands clear of the panel.

CONTROLS and TRAINING

  • Both open inside the panel, in place of the leaderboard. The button stays pressed in and its arrow turns down; click it again or ✕ CLOSE to go back.
  • BREAK OUT stays where it is, so you can start a run from either view.
  • CONTROLS keeps the game's grouping (on a roof / in the air, then the two materials). KEYBOARD / TOUCH swaps the keys for the mobile button names, like touchUi does now.
  • TRAINING lists the 8 lessons and the final test before you start. The game only remembers "training finished", so showing ✓ per lesson would need new saved progress.

Last run

  • Logged by the system, so it fits the story: every failed escape gets a log entry.
  • On someone's first visit it would say "no previous iterations".
  • "vs best" turns gold and says NEW BEST when the last run set one.

Transition (the ▶ button)

  • As Bob hits the firewall, the panel slides out to the left and leaves a thin ITER 0042 · LIVE strip.
  • In the game that strip could stay as part of the HUD, so the menu turns into the HUD instead of just vanishing.

Leaderboard

  • The week's top 10 is the main list, since that's the one you can still climb. The all-time top 3 sits above it as the hall of fame.
  • If you're outside the week's top 10, a ⋯ and your own row are added under #10 (try the toggle).
  • The top 3 ranks are gold, silver and bronze in both lists.

Short screen

  • On short or wide screens the flavour lines in the log go first. The last-run report and Bob's line stay, so the buttons and records always fit.
  • Portrait phones use the bottom sheet (iteration D in start-screen-shell.html).
