The layout

  • The title block is the same as in 01. Under it, one terminal window sits on the sky down to the roof line, so START is still the brightest thing on screen.
  • Left pane: the system log (the last iteration, the subject being loaded, Bob's line). Under it is YOUR BEST with the bar to #1, which was in 01's board before.
  • Right pane: the board with 01's WEEK / ALL-TIME tabs, 8 rows each, and the purge timer. Your row is marked in cyan, so your score isn't printed twice.
  • The terminal's title bar shows whose shell it is: system://sector-00 · subject MANIMAC.

Where TERMINATE goes

  • Terminal bar (suggested).
  • It sits right next to the name it terminates. It's part of the system's window, not a help button. And it's away from TRAINING / CONTROLS, so a new player looking for help won't click it.
  • Your best: it reads as "this is you, and you can end it". But it's small and low down, and easy to miss.
  • Button row: the easiest to find, but it puts a destructive action between the two help buttons. The 1.2 s hold stops accidents, but it still looks like a menu choice everyone should try.
  • In all three, the confirm prints in the log pane, in the system's voice. The board stays in view, so you can see your records will stay.

After a terminate

  • The frame glitches out and boots back up. The kicker reads ITERATION 0001 and the log opens on subject MANIMAC TERMINATED.
  • The subject is BOB again, marked unregistered in amber, and TERMINATE is hidden until the next record gives Bob a name.
  • MANIMAC's rows stay on the board but lose the cyan "you" mark.
