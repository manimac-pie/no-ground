Drop-down console v2

Iteration C from start-screen-shell.html, updated with the notes in StartScreensUpdate.md.

What changed

  1. Bob's line is in the console log: [00.09] BOB: let me out. The system's "unexpected input" line moves to [00.08], just before it, and the speech bubble over Bob says the same line.
  2. The command chips are now buttons: raised like keys, with an icon, an arrow, a hover glow and a pressed state. BREAK OUT is filled cyan so it's clearly the main one. Hovering a button "types" its command into the prompt.
  3. Subject records:
    • The purge timer is back to hhh:mm:ss and counts down live.
    • Your best score is now shown (YOUR BEST), with the gap to #1 next to it at a readable size.
    • Names are shown with a YOU tag on your row: Manimac YOU
    • The ALL tab works. Click it in the frame, or compare both below.

How ALL differs

  • Top 3 of all time, then ⋯ and your own row if you're outside the top 3, so you always see where you stand.
  • The bar and "to #1" measure against the all-time #1.
  • No purge timer (all-time is never purged). The footer says how long the board has been running instead.

Open questions

  • Should YOUR BEST on the WEEK tab be your best this week or your best ever? Here it's this week's.
